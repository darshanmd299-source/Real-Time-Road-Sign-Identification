Engineering Overview

About BharatSign AI

Pioneering real-time computer vision technology tailored for Indian road infrastructure, ADAS safety, and traffic enforcement.

🚀

1. Project Introduction

BharatSign AI is a sub-12ms deep learning computer vision system engineered for Indian road traffic sign identification and collision avoidance integrating IRC:67-2012 guidelines.

⚠️

2. Problem Statement

Indian highways present challenging vision environments—occlusion from heavy trucks, dust, monsoon fog, and non-standard mounting heights.

🎯

3. Objectives

Deploy end-to-end CNN architecture targeting >99% mAP accuracy at 120 FPS on edge hardware with legal fine warnings.

🔬

4. Methodology

Pipeline passes 1080p camera frames through CLAHE filtering, single-pass YOLOv8 bounding box regression, and TensorRT FP16 quantization.

🧠

5. CNN Architecture

YOLOv8 backbone fused with MobileNetV3 inverted residual blocks and Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN) for micro-sign detection.

⚙️

6. Working Principle

Camera stream triggers bounding box anchors matching 48 IRC class embeddings in sub-12ms with warning speech chimes.

🌐

7. Future Scope & Autonomous Vehicle Integration

3D LiDAR point cloud fusion, V2X cellular broadcasting, automated speed-governor throttling, and NHAI smart toll plaza integration.
