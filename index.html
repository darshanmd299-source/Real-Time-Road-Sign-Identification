Engineering Overview

About BharatSign AI

Pioneering real-time computer vision technology tailored for Indian road infrastructure, ADAS safety, and traffic enforcement.

🚀

1. Project Introduction

BharatSign AI is a sub-12ms deep learning computer vision system engineered specifically for Indian road traffic sign identification and collision avoidance.

⚠️

2. Problem Statement

Indian highways present challenging vision environments—occlusion from heavy trucks, non-standard sign heights, dust, and monsoon fog.

🎯

3. Objectives

Deploy an end-to-end CNN architecture targeting >99% mAP accuracy at 120 FPS on edge hardware, auto-generating legal fine warnings.

🔬

4. Methodology

Our pipeline passes 1080p camera frames through CLAHE filtering, runs single-pass CNN bounding box regression, and executes TensorRT FP16 quantization.

🧠

5. CNN Architecture

Leveraging a hybrid YOLOv8 backbone fused with MobileNetV3 inverted residual blocks for micro-sign detection at high speeds.

⚙️

6. Working Principle

The camera stream triggers bounding box candidate anchors matching 48 IRC class embeddings in sub-12ms, triggering warning speech chimes.

🌐

7. Future Scope & Autonomous Vehicle Integration

Future expansion includes 3D LiDAR point cloud fusion, V2X cellular network broadcasting, and direct integration with NHAI smart toll plazas.
